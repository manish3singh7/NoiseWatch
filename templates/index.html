🔊 Is Your Area Too Noisy?

Understanding decibel levels and their impact on your health.

🟢 Below 55 dB

Safe

Comfortable for conversation, studying, and restorative sleep.

Example: Quiet library, residential park.

🟡 55–75 dB

Moderate

Acceptable during daytime, but may cause disturbance at night.

Example: Regular traffic, busy office, restaurant.

🟠 75–85 dB

High

Potential stress and fatigue with prolonged exposure.

Example: Heavy transit highway, construction work.

🔴 Above 85 dB

Critical

Harmful sound levels that can cause hearing damage over time.

Example: Horns at close range, rock concert, drill.

About NoiseWatch

NoiseWatch is an open civic technology initiative created to address urban noise pollution in high-density corridors. Developed for modern smart-city governance, our mission is to empower citizens with actionable acoustic data.
